Questions about selling candles with Vendl

Can I calculate the cost of each candle size?

Yes. Enter batch materials, labour and overheads, set saleable yield for that size, then add packaging per candle.

Can I sell different scents and sizes?

Yes. Create products or options for scent and size.

Can I accept pre-orders for a new collection?

Yes. Open a pre-order for a scent release, take payment, then fulfil when that batch is ready.

Can I sell at markets as well as online?

Yes. Use QR checkout for stall sales alongside your online shop link or website.

What should I check before shipping candles to customers?

Vendl helps you take and fulfil orders. Packaging, carrier rules and destination restrictions are your decisions.

Can I start selling without building a full website?

Yes. Free includes a shop link. Pro adds website, custom domain and costing tools.

What does Vendl cost, including transaction fees?

Start free. Pro unlocks website, costing and email. Card processing fees apply separately.
