Questions about finding your page

Is Vendl only for home businesses?

No. Vendl is for independent makers and small production businesses working in kitchens, studios or workshops.

Do I need a website to start?

Free provides a shop link. Pro adds a full website and custom-domain connection.

What if my product is not listed?

Browse the closest category or start free and set up the products you make. We do not auto-create new indexable pages from search.
