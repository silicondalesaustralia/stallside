Frequently asked questions.

Can I calculate the cost of each candle size?

Yes. Enter the batch materials, labour and overheads, set the saleable yield for that size, then add packaging per candle.

Can I sell different scents and sizes?

Yes. Create products or options for scent and size.

Can I sell online and at markets?

Yes. Use QR checkout for stall sales alongside your shop link or website.

Can I accept pre-orders for a new collection?

Yes. Open a pre-order for a scent release, take payment and fulfil when the batch is ready.

Can I move my existing products?

Import supported product and customer data from supported sources. Imports do not recreate your website design, pages or subscriptions.

Do I need a full website to start?

No. Free includes your shop link. Pro adds a website, custom domain and costing tools.
